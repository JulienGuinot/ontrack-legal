Privacy Policy — OnTrack

Last updated: September 1, 2026. Version française

1. Who we are

OnTrack is a local delivery application in Senegal that connects customers who need a package delivered with nearby available drivers. The data controller is Julien Guinot. For any question regarding your personal data, you can contact us at julien.guinot01@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect the following data as part of the operation of the application:

  • Your phone number, used for SMS authentication via our provider MTarget.
  • Your name and profile photo.
  • For drivers only: identity documents (national ID card, front and back, plus a selfie) required for identity verification (KYC).
  • Your precise location while you are an online driver, including in the background during an active delivery.
  • The pickup and drop-off addresses associated with your orders.
  • Messages exchanged in the chat associated with a delivery.
  • Payment information, processed directly by our provider SamirPay. OnTrack never stores mobile money account numbers, except for the payout number provided by the driver to receive their earnings.
  • A notification device identifier (Expo Push token), used only to send you alerts about your deliveries.
  • The addresses you type in the search bar, sent to Google to return suggestions.

3. Why we collect this data

This data is used to provide the service (matching customers and drivers, real-time delivery tracking, processing payments and payouts), to ensure the security of the platform (in particular, driver identity verification), and to comply with our accounting obligations (keeping a history of transactions).

4. Background location

Background location is only used for drivers, and only while they are online or on an active delivery. A persistent notification always indicates that location tracking is active. This tracking stops automatically as soon as the driver goes offline.

5. Data sharing

We share certain data with the following providers, strictly as part of operating the service:

  • SamirPay (payment processing and driver payouts).
  • MTarget (authentication SMS delivery).
  • Supabase (database and file hosting).
  • Google — Places, Geocoding, Routes and map tiles: the addresses you type and the pickup/drop-off coordinates are sent to Google for autocomplete, address/coordinate conversion, route computation and map display.
  • Expo (Expo Push Notifications): delivery of notifications to your device.
  • MiX Telematics: for drivers using an OnTrack fleet vehicle, vehicle positions come from this provider.

5b. Transfers outside Senegal

Our hosting, mapping and notification providers (Supabase, Google, Expo) process data outside Senegal, in particular in the European Union and the United States. These transfers are limited to the data needed for the purposes described above.

We never sell your data to third parties and never use it for advertising purposes.

6. Retention period

Profile data and KYC documents are kept for the lifetime of your account. Order history and the payment ledger are kept for accounting purposes even after account deletion, but attached to an anonymized profile. Location data is continuously overwritten: only the last known position is kept.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app, under Profile > Delete my account. This deletes your login credentials, KYC documents, profile photo, last known location and notification tokens, and anonymizes your profile. Your order history and the associated financial ledger are kept, in anonymized form, to meet our accounting obligations.

Deletion is held back while a delivery is in progress, or while a payout or refund is still outstanding: we must be able to close the financial operation before erasing the account.

8. Security

We implement technical security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), strict row-level access control (Row Level Security) on our database, and server-side-only secret management.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, and delete your data. You can exercise these rights directly from the app, or by contacting us by email at julien.guinot01@gmail.com.
